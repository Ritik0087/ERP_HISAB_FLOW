About HisabFlow

Local business suite · Version 3.1

Offline-first. Your data stays in this app's local SQLite database. Sign-in accounts are stored only on this computer — no internet or cloud account is needed. A dated SQLite backup is also created locally when the app starts. Barcode/QR camera scanning needs a supported browser and camera permission; USB scanners can type into the scan field.
This prototype does not file GST returns, generate e-way bills or register IRNs/e-invoices. WhatsApp actions open a pre-filled message for you to review and send manually. Check invoice requirements with your GST professional before business use.
